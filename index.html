Research Overview

We aim to build a systematic framework for closed-loop intelligent decision planning of unmanned platforms in complex dynamic interactive scenarios. The research addresses key challenges such as insufficient interactive data, unrealistic behavior logic in simulation, difficult risk prediction, limited policy generalization, unstable control under complex motion conditions, and sim-to-real deployment gaps.

Interactive Scenario Construction → Risk Prediction and Environmental Reasoning → Reinforcement Learning Decision Planning → Complex Motion Control → Sim-to-Real Validation
Direction 01

Closed-loop Interactive Simulation and Scenario Generation

We construct simulation and generative systems with behavioral plausibility, visual realism, physical consistency, and editability. This direction provides high-quality training and validation environments for downstream risk reasoning, decision learning, and sim-to-real transfer.

Direction 02

Reasoning-enhanced Decision Planning in Dense Interactions

We study how autonomous vehicles and robots can make safe, efficient, robust, and interpretable decisions under multi-agent interaction, incomplete information, uncertain risk, and mixed cooperative-competitive relationships.

RL Control and Cross-platform Adaptation

Research Topics

Direction 01 · Closed-loop Interactive Simulation and Scenario Generation

Pedestrian-Vehicle Dynamic Interactive Parking Scenario Construction
DynaPark-Ped · Pedestrian-aware autonomous parking · Closed-loop validation

Brief: We construct pedestrian-vehicle interactive parking scenarios and the DynaPark-Ped dataset, where pedestrians can respond to vehicle motion. The dataset provides surround-view images, LiDAR, target states, bounding boxes, and other multimodal annotations for closed-loop dynamic parking evaluation.

Role: This topic provides the foundational interactive simulation environment for studying autonomous parking under dynamic pedestrian disturbance.

Editable Closed-loop Scenario Generation Based on Digital Assets
CARLA · NuRec · Object-Conditioned Generative Field · Photorealistic scenario editing

Brief: We study object-conditioned generative fields for CARLA and NuRec-based scene editing. By using asset geometry, rigid-body motion, illumination priors, and reconstructed backgrounds, the model generates local appearance residuals, shadows, and contact-consistent details within object ROIs.

Role: This topic addresses how realistic closed-loop scenarios can be edited, generated, and physically integrated with reconstructed environments.

Interaction Causal Logic Modeling and Script-driven Scenario Generation
VLM-assisted annotation · Causal interaction logic · Behavior-realistic simulation

Brief: We model causal chains among vehicle actions, pedestrian responses, body movements, facial expressions, and behavioral intentions through vision-language models, statistical modeling, and self-correction mechanisms. The resulting editable scripts drive behavior-realistic dynamic agents in closed-loop simulators.

Role: This topic provides the behavior-level realism needed for pedestrians and vehicles to react like real-world agents in simulation.

Intelligent Scene Recognition and Image Enhancement
Low-light enhancement · Rain/fog removal · Glare suppression · Risk object detection

Brief: We develop algorithms for blind-spot image enhancement, low-light denoising, dehazing, deraining, glare suppression, collision-risk object detection, dynamic behavior prediction, and road condition recognition under adverse visual conditions.

Role: This topic supports robust perception front-ends for closed-loop simulation, risk reasoning, and real vehicle deployment.

Direction 02 · Reasoning-enhanced Decision Planning in Dense Interactions

Prediction-Planning Coupled Interactive Decision Framework
Ego-conditioned prediction · Dynamic voxels · Closed-loop predictive planning

Brief: We study ego-conditioned interactive prediction and dynamic voxel-based planning. Ego candidate maneuvers are used as conditions for predicting other agents, while predicted futures are converted into spatio-temporal dynamic voxels for risk-aware trajectory evaluation and planning guidance.

Role: This topic shifts autonomous driving from serial “prediction-then-planning” to closed-loop “prediction-planning mutual feedback”.

Game-guided Multi-agent Interactive Decision-making
Stackelberg game · Priority reasoning · MAPPO · Mixed traffic interaction

Brief: We introduce Stackelberg game priors into multi-agent reinforcement learning for strong-conflict scenarios such as narrow-road meeting. Priority estimation identifies leader/follower roles, which condition MAPPO policies. Preference-IDM mixed traffic and self-play adversarial learning are used to improve robustness.

Role: This topic addresses negotiation, yielding, and deadlock avoidance in multi-agent conflict scenarios.

Risk-aware Safe Reinforcement Learning Decision-making
Optimistic-pessimistic dual actors · Risk field · Human-in-the-loop adversarial disturbance

Brief: We develop risk-aware optimistic-pessimistic dual-actor reinforcement learning. Predicted trajectories and risk fields identify high-risk modes; the optimistic actor pursues efficiency, while the pessimistic actor focuses on safety. Adaptive fusion generates final decisions and is evaluated under adversarial human takeover.

Role: This topic balances safety and efficiency in complex interaction without becoming overly aggressive or overly conservative.

Task-driven Decision-making for Human-Vehicle and Human-Robot Interaction
Pedestrian-aware parking · User-preference navigation · Embodied interactive decision-making

Brief: We integrate pedestrian prediction, visual cues, planning control, and closed-loop validation into dynamic parking tasks. We further extend from traffic-rule/risk-driven decisions to embodied robot decisions guided by user language, behavior preferences, and task requirements.

Role: This topic expands decision planning from vehicle-vehicle interaction to human-vehicle and human-robot task collaboration.

Dangerous Scenario Cognition with Knowledge Graphs and Large Language Models
Multimodal perception · Semantic graph · Memory recall · LLM reasoning

Brief: We build a danger cognition system that integrates multimodal perception, semantic knowledge graphs, memory recall, and large-model reasoning. It simulates the human process of observing, associating, recalling, and judging to infer invisible hazards and potential conflict chains.

Role: This topic moves risk prediction from purely data-driven correlation toward knowledge-enhanced reasoning and counterfactual cognition.

Memory-enhanced Autonomous Driving Decision Planning
Geo-Risk Memory Graph · Spatial risk prior · Dynamic risk evolution · Driving experience retrieval

Brief: We study Geo-Risk Memory Graphs that unify road structure, geographic environment, historical risk events, dynamic interaction evolution, and executable driving experience. The goal is to retrieve not only similar trajectories, but also similar geographic risk structures and effective handling strategies.

Role: This topic enables autonomous vehicles to remember dangerous places, recall past experience, and generalize risk-aware decisions across scenarios.

Language-guided Humanoid Portrait Photography via Viewpoint Policy Learning
PhotoAgent · Composition constraints · Humanoid robot · User preference

Brief: We translate user photography instructions into composition constraints and learn viewpoint selection, body posture, and visual micro-adjustment policies for humanoid robots. The robot uses camera images, human position, depth, and candidate viewpoints to capture photos satisfying user preference and aesthetic requirements.

Role: This topic extends decision planning from navigation and collision avoidance to language-guided, preference-aware embodied service tasks.

Direction 03 · Reinforcement Learning Control and Cross-platform Adaptation

Physics-adaptive Curriculum Reinforcement Learning for Extreme-speed Racing
Autonomous racing · Curvature-aware reward · TTC switching · Progressive curriculum

Brief: We study Physics-Adaptive Curriculum Reinforcement Learning for extreme-speed autonomous racing. The method integrates geometry-enhanced states, curvature-aware physics-adaptive rewards, TTC-triggered safety switching, and progressive curriculum learning from stable driving to high-speed cornering and adversarial overtaking.

Role: This topic demonstrates stable reinforcement learning control under high-speed, high-dynamic, and high-risk motion conditions.

Robust Localization and Stable Control for Autonomous Racing Deployment
LiDAR localization · RMPC-F control · Abu Dhabi Autonomous Racing League

Brief: We develop LiDAR-centered real-time robust localization and RMPC-F-based stable control for high-speed autonomous racing. The system supports high-frequency localization, trajectory tracking, velocity control, and robust deployment on real racing platforms.

Role: This topic provides the real-world deployment and engineering validation layer for autonomous racing algorithms.

RS Cost Field-guided Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Parking
Reeds-Shepp prior · Learnable cost field · Dual critic · Narrow-space parking

Brief: We build learnable Reeds-Shepp cost fields to encode vehicle kinematic feasibility and environment-dependent maneuverability. The learned RS prior is integrated into reward design and dual-critic reinforcement learning to improve parking success, compactness, and generalization in parallel, vertical, slanted, and narrow parking scenarios.

Role: This topic addresses efficient policy learning under strong kinematic constraints and narrow-space motion requirements.

Safe Constrained Reinforcement Learning for Complex Motion Platforms
CMDP · CPO · PPO-Lagrangian · Vehicle dynamics shield

Brief: We formulate autonomous racing and complex motion control as constrained Markov decision processes and introduce constrained reinforcement learning algorithms with vehicle-dynamics-based safety shields. Unsafe speed commands are projected to safe boundaries to coordinate safety and performance.

Role: This topic provides safety-control foundations for later racing, high-speed deployment, and complex motion tasks.

Cross-platform Universal Adaptive Controller
Base dynamics · Online residual learning · Meta adaptation · Hardware acceleration

Brief: We study a software-hardware integrated universal adaptive controller that combines base dynamics, online residual learning, meta-learning-based fast adaptation, and dedicated computing hardware. The goal is to compensate for payload variation, model changes, environmental disturbance, and heterogeneous platform differences with low latency.

Role: This topic extends from single-platform vehicle control to reusable, adaptive, and cross-platform control cores for heterogeneous unmanned systems.

Adaptive Control for Sim-to-real, Cross-platform, and Cross-morphology Transfer
Meta-RL · Skill distillation · Universal-specific skill disentanglement · TRON1 morphology transfer

Brief: We study meta reinforcement learning, skill distillation, and universal-specific skill disentanglement for transferring control experience across simulators, real platforms, and robot morphologies. Universal skills capture task-relevant structures, while morphology-specific skills adapt contact, support, friction, and dynamics strategies.

Role: This topic answers how motion experience learned by one platform or morphology can be efficiently absorbed and reconstructed by another.
