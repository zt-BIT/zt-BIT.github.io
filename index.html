Research Overview

Our research is organized around a unified theme: closed-loop intelligent decision planning for unmanned systems in complex interactive environments. We study how to construct realistic interactive simulation environments, how to learn safe and robust decision-making policies, and how to achieve stable control and fast adaptation under complex or extreme motion conditions.

Direction 01

Closed-loop Interactive Simulation and Generative Scenario Construction

We build simulation and generative systems with visual realism, physical consistency, and behavior-level interaction. Representative topics include pedestrian-aware parking simulation, object-conditioned photorealistic generation, interaction logic modeling, and scenario enhancement under adverse visual conditions.

DynaPark-Ped OCGF Interaction Logic
Direction 02

Reasoning-enhanced Decision Planning in Dense Interactive Scenarios

We study prediction-planning coupling, risk-aware reinforcement learning, game-guided multi-agent decision-making, and human/vehicle interaction-aware planning. The goal is to enable autonomous vehicles and robots to make safe, efficient, and socially compatible decisions in dense, uncertain, and mixed cooperative-competitive environments.

Dynamic Voxels ROP-DARL Game-guided MARL
Direction 03

Reinforcement Learning Control and Cross-platform Adaptation

We develop physics-informed reinforcement learning, constrained control, curriculum learning, sim-to-real transfer, and adaptive residual dynamics learning for autonomous racing, autonomous parking, mobile robots, and legged robots. The long-term goal is to build reusable and adaptive control cores for heterogeneous unmanned platforms.

Ongoing and Future Research

Object-Conditioned Generative Field for Physically Consistent Scenario Editing
Ongoing Project
Closed-loop simulation · CARLA · NuRec · Object-level photorealistic generation

We aim to convert simulator assets, rigid-body motion, and illumination priors into a differentiable local generator that produces appearance residuals, shadows, and contact-consistent details within object ROIs.

Causal Interaction Logic Modeling for Vehicle-Pedestrian Scenario Generation
Ongoing Project
VLM-assisted annotation · Interaction scripts · Behavior-realistic simulation

We study how to describe, annotate, and generate realistic vehicle-pedestrian interaction processes, including vehicle motion states, pedestrian actions, body movements, and causal response logic.

Cross-platform Universal Adaptive Controller for Heterogeneous Unmanned Systems
Ongoing Project
Base dynamics · Online residual learning · Meta adaptation · Hardware acceleration

We develop a reusable control core that combines prior dynamics, online residual modeling, meta-learning, and customized computing hardware for fast adaptation across heterogeneous robots and unmanned platforms.
